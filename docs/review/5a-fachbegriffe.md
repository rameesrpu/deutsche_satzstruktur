# Medical content — not yet checked by a doctor

**Fachbegriffe und Alltagswörter: review sheet (plan 5a, Task 3)**
*Medical terms and everyday words: review sheet*

Board `fachbegriffe` ("Fachsprache und Alltagssprache", Üben → Bilder and Klinik). Data: `data/boards/fachbegriffe.json`, version 2, **51 pairs in 8 sets**. Modes: Paare (match tiles), Für den Patienten (Fachbegriff → Alltagswort), Für die Kollegin (Alltagswort → Fachbegriff). Checked 2026-11-21.

Gelesen / read for each pair: **DocCheck Flexikon** (term, synonym lines, lay-word pages). The articles were confirmed with **Duden / DWDS** where the medical dictionary does not print them (ruling R5). **Pschyrembel online could not be used**: every entry returns a login page (subscription only). Twin = the opposite term used as a harder wrong answer.

**Bitte prüfen / Please check:** that each pair is what a doctor on a German ward would say, and the open points at the end. A pair that is wrong should be removed, not corrected from memory.

## Dropped or changed pairs

| Pair | What happened | Why |
|---|---|---|
| das Karzinom — der Krebs | **Dropped** | "Krebs" is the umbrella term for all malignant tumours; a Karzinom is only the epithelial kind, so the pair would teach a false equation (DocCheck Krebs, Karzinom). |
| die Thrombose — das Blutgerinnsel | **Changed** to der Thrombus — das Blutgerinnsel | A Thrombose is the process, the Thrombus is the clot (DocCheck Thrombus lists "Blutgerinnsel" as synonym). |
| die Rhinitis | Alltagswort Nasenschleimhautentzündung, then back to **der Schnupfen** | DocCheck: Schnupfen is the lay word for infectious Rhinitis. |
| die Zephalgie | Kopfweh, then back to **die Kopfschmerzen (pl.)** | DocCheck lists Zephalgie as a synonym of Kopfschmerz; Duden: mostly plural. |
| die Synkope | Kreislaufkollaps, then back to **die Ohnmacht** | DocCheck Bewusstlosigkeit: "Synonyme … Ohnmacht"; Synkope = faint from reduced brain blood flow. |
| die Adipositas | Kept as die Fettleibigkeit | "starkes Übergewicht" is not printed as a synonym. |
| Hypotonie, Apoplex, Nephrolithiasis, Injektion | Dropped in the first pass, **restored** | Evidence found in the second pass (see tables above). |
| Schnupfen, Ohnmacht as separate pairs | Not added | Used as Alltagswort of Rhinitis / Synkope instead. |

## Offene Punkte / Open points

1. **Definition only, no printed lay synonym**: Hypotonie = niedriger Blutdruck, Hämaturie = Blut im Urin, Insomnie = Schlafstörung rest on the DocCheck definition (and Duden's meaning line), not on a listed everyday word. Probably right, but ask a doctor.
2. **Appendizitis — Blinddarmentzündung**: DocCheck lists it as "fälschlich auch Blinddarmentzündung" (anatomically imprecise: the Blinddarm is the Caecum). Kept because it is the word patients use.
3. **Articles**: for about ten terms (Hepatitis, Emesis, Hypertonie, Myokardinfarkt, Anämie, Dyspnoe, Rhinitis, Sinusitis, Appendizitis, Ikterus) DocCheck prints no article; they are confirmed only by grammar rules and Duden/DWDS, not by a medical dictionary.
4. **Apoplex** is marked "veraltet" (outdated) by DocCheck; still heard on wards.
5. **Pschyrembel** was not reachable without a login. If she has access, a quick pass over the 51 pairs against it would close the gap.
6. The sets "Niere und Blase" (3 items) and others are small; a round never mixes sets.
