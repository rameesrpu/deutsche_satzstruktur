NOUNS - COLOR = GENDER:
Blue = Masculine (♂)
Pink = Feminine (♀)
Dark Violet = Neuter (⚲)
NOUNS - SHAPE = CASE:
Circle = Nominative
Square = Accusative
Trapezoid = Dative
Diamond = Genitive
PRONOUNS - COLOR: ich, du, er♂, sie♀, es⚲, wir, ihr, sie/Sie(pl.)
Red color = Plural nouns/adjectives (uses 'sie' plural color)
Hexagon = Verb/Modal
Pentagon = Subordinating Conj. (weil, dass, etc.)
Star = Coordinating Conj. (und, aber, etc.)
PREPOSITIONS:
Triangle = Dative Prep.
Small Square = Accusative Prep.
Small Diamond = Genitive Prep.
Parallelogram = Wechsel Prep.
Octagon = Other
